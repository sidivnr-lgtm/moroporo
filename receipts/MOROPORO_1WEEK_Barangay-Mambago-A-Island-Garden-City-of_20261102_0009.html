5 · Implications

HINGA Extreme Caution · 7 days
Schools: Suspend face-to-face classes and reduce academic demand.
Health: Heat cramps and heat exhaustion become likely with prolonged exposure; prioritise water, shade and rest.
Actions: Light learning tasks · Learning packets · DepEd TV / radio · Well-being check-ins
LABOR & WORKPLACE: Enforce 15-minute shaded rest cycles per working hour on days at 33 °C or more. (cutoffs set in the app; verify against the current DOLE advisory)
MODEL-ESTIMATED IMPACTS (by 2040): outdoor labor capacity loss 8.8% · hospital ER heat-surge risk +247.8%
