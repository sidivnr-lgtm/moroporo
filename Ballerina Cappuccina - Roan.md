**Color Separation Challenge (Water, Acetone, Salt)**



**The experiment shows how liquids with different polarities can separate into layers when salt is added to water. As acetone becomes less soluble in salty water, dyes redistribute based on which liquid they dissolve in best. The visible colored layers demonstrate intermolecular forces and polarity preference.**



**Process / Science Behind It**

**Step Process**



**1. Mix colored solution with water**

**2. Add acetone → forms one mixture**

**3. Add salt → water becomes more polar**

**4. Acetone separates from water**

**5. Colors move into preferred layer**



**Scientific Explanation**



**Salt causes salting-out effect (reduces acetone solubility)**



**Water = highly polar**

**Acetone = moderately polar**

**Dyes follow “like dissolves like” rule**

**Separation caused by intermolecular attractions, not reaction**





















**2. Density Experiment (Honey, Soap, Oil)**

**Science Behind It Density determines position in a column**



**More dense → sinks**

**Less dense → floats**

**Polar vs non-polar liquids don’t mix**

**Viscosity affects pouring speed**



**Approximate Densities**

**Honey ≈ 1.36 g/mL (heaviest)**

**Dish soap ≈ 1.03–1.10 g/mL**

**Oil ≈ 0.90 g/mL (lightest)**



**👉 Therefore the layers form: Bottom — Honey**

**Middle — Soap**

**Top — Oil**



**What Happens Physically**



**Honey and liquid soap are polar, so their molecules attract each other and stay together, while oil is non-polar and does not mix with them. Because polar and non-polar substances repel each other, the liquids remain separated and form layers instead of blending.**





**---**



**Molecular Explanation**



**Honey: concentrated sugars → tightly packed molecules → very dense**

**Soap: amphiphilic molecules → moderate density**

**Oil: nonpolar hydrocarbons → loosely packed → low density**



**Because water-based substances (honey \& soap) are polar and oil is nonpolar, they are immiscible, so they stay separated instead of blending.**
